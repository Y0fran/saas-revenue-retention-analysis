# SaaS Revenue & Customer Retention Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyzes a fictional B2B SaaS business to understand its recurring revenue, customer behavior, subscription performance, and retention patterns.

The goal is to transform raw business data into actionable insights that support strategic decision-making.

## Business Question

Is the company growing sustainably, and what actions should it take to improve recurring revenue and customer retention over the next six months?

## Objectives

- Analyze recurring revenue performance (MRR).
- Understand customer and subscription distribution.
- Identify customer churn patterns.
- Evaluate revenue contribution by subscription plan.
- Investigate customer retention and cohort behavior.
- Develop data-driven business recommendations.

## Tools & Technologies

- PostgreSQL
- SQL
- pgAdmin 4
- Git and GitHub

## Dataset

The project uses a synthetic B2B SaaS subscription analytics dataset.

Source: [B2B SaaS Subscription Analytics](https://www.misata.studio/datasets/saas-subscription-analytics)
